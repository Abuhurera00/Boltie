# Workflow

How we keep this vault useful instead of a pile of stale notes.

every session
- open today's log (daily note, it lands in `Log/`). Write what you did, where you got stuck, what's next. Three short lists, nothing fancy.
- tick things in [[Roadmap]] as they ship. Add new lines when you find work, don't keep it in your head.

when you make a call that someone could reasonably question later
- new note in `Decisions/` from the Decision template. Number it. Link it from [[Home]].
- keep it short. Why we had to decide, what we picked, what we gave up.
- if a decision gets reversed, don't delete it. Mark it `status: replaced by [[NNNN ...]]`.

when you look at another project or a library
- note in `Research/`. What it is, what we learned, what we'll steal or avoid. Write down what you actually saw, not what you guessed.

for AI agents working in this repo
- read [[Home]], [[Architecture]] and [[Roadmap]] before changing code
- add a line to today's log when you finish something
- don't rewrite old log entries

style
- plain markdown, short bullets, lowercase headings are fine
- no diagrams unless a picture really is clearer than three bullets
- link notes with `[[wikilinks]]`
